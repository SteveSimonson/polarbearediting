FAQ

Straight answers

What kinds of book editing do you offer?

Developmental editing, copyediting, proofreading, manuscript critiques, and formatting for ebook and print. Most first-time clients start with a critique or sample edit.

How much does professional book editing cost?

Industry ranges often land around $0.01–$0.06 per word depending on the pass. Use our cost calculator for a ballpark, then request a scoped quote for your manuscript.

When should I hire a book editor?

After you have taken the book as far as you can. Developmental after serious revision (often post-beta); copyediting after structure is locked; proofreading last. Details in when to hire an editor.

Is this only for self-publishing authors?

No. Indie, hybrid, and pre-agent authors all use professional editing. Traditional paths still benefit from clean opening pages and a structurally sound full manuscript.
