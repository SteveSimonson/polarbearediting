Book formatting for ebook & print

Professional book formatting for Kindle/ebook and print interiors—clean typography, stable files, upload-ready packages.

Great books still die in ugly files. Formatting is production craft: readable type, sensible hierarchy, stable navigation, and exports that survive KDP and wide distributors.

What we deliver

  • Ebook interior (reflowable) optimized for major stores
  • Print-ready interior PDF to your trim size
  • Front/back matter structure: title, copyright, TOC, about the author
  • Consistent styles for chapter openers, letters, texts, scene breaks

Before you format

Finish content edits first. Formatting a moving target multiplies cost. Ideally: developmental → copyedit → format → proof.

Next step: Prefer a lighter start? A manuscript critique or sample pages often clarify whether you need developmental work or can move straight to copyediting.
