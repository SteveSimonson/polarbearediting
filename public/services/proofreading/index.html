Proofreading for the final clean pass

Professional book proofreading to catch typos, punctuation issues, and production errors after copyediting and layout freeze.

Proofreading is not a cheaper developmental edit. It is the last quality gate: residual typos, punctuation slips, repeated words, bad breaks, and “how did that survive?” glitches.

When proofreading makes sense

  • After copyediting (or an equivalent high-quality pass)
  • After interior formatting for print/ebook when possible
  • Before you hit publish—not while you are still rewriting Chapter 12

What we catch

  • Spelling, punctuation, and basic grammar misses
  • Formatting glitches: extra spaces, odd hyphens, style breakouts
  • Obvious continuity leftovers if they surface in the final file

If the manuscript still needs structural or heavy line work, we will recommend stepping back a stage rather than proofreading a draft that will change.

FAQ

Can proofreading replace copyediting?

No. Proofreading assumes the text is already stable. Skipping copyedit to “just proof” usually produces an elegant mess.

Next step: Prefer a lighter start? A manuscript critique or sample pages often clarify whether you need developmental work or can move straight to copyediting.
