When is the best time to hire a book editor?

Hire a book editor after serious self-revision—developmental after betas, copyedit after structure locks, proofread last. Timing guide for indie authors.

This is one of the original Polar Bear questions—and it still separates authors who get ROI from authors who fund rewrite loops.

Hire a developmental editor when

  • You finished a complete draft and revised it hard yourself
  • Beta readers agree on big issues (or contradict in ways you cannot resolve)
  • You are too close to the book to see structure clearly

Hire a copyeditor when

  • Plot/argument is stable—you are not planning major scene surgery
  • You want a professional language pass before formatting or agent submission

Hire a proofreader when

  • Copyedit is done and revisions from that pass are implemented
  • Preferably after interior layout for print/ebook

Ways to start smaller

First-five-pages or sample chapter reviews test chemistry. A manuscript critique is ideal when you need direction more than full markup.

The journey of a thousand miles begins with, “But I’m too tiirreedddd. I’ll just start tomorrow.” — early Polar Bear motto energy, still true

Hire when you are ready to act on feedback—not when you only want reassurance.
