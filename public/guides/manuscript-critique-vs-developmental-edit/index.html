Manuscript critique vs developmental edit: which first?

Compare manuscript critiques and developmental edits—depth, cost, deliverables, and when each is the smarter buy for your draft.

Manuscript critiqueDevelopmental edit
DepthDiagnostic overviewDeep structural collaboration
Typical deliverableEditorial letterLetter + more granular guidance / comments
CostLowerHigher
Best forDirection, triage, fit testMajor revision with professional partner

Choose a critique when

  • You need a professional read before committing to a full developmental budget
  • You want prioritized issues, not full markup
  • You are between drafts and deciding what to tackle next

Choose developmental when

  • You already know the book needs structural work
  • Betas conflict and you need authoritative prioritization
  • The manuscript is close but not launch-ready at the story level
