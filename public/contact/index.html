Tell us about the manuscript

Email is the fastest path. Include genre, word count, stage (draft 2, post-beta, pre-upload), deadline, and the service you think you need.

Email the desk

We reply with a stage recommendation, sample-page plan, and timing. If we are not the right fit, we will say so.

hello@polarbearediting.com

Prefer structure? Paste this checklist into your email:

  • Working title + genre/subgenre
  • Word count
  • Fiction or nonfiction
  • What you want help with
  • Deadline or launch target
  • Whether you have beta feedback
